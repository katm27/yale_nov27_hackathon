# FEWS NET staple food prices, South Sudan (weekly, 2021-06 onward)

- Dataset page: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/fewsnet_staple_food_price_data_for_south_sudan_weekly_6857
- File: https://fdw.fews.net/api/marketpricefacts/?dataset=FEWS_NET_Staple_Food_Price_Data&country=SS&format=csv&fields=website
  (FEWS NET Data Warehouse public API, no login), accessed 2026-11-17. 61,308 rows, 18 markets, 2021-06-11 .. 2026-10-23.
- Used by scripts/wfp_filled.py (gap-filled WFP price measures) for local sorghum (Feterita, 3.5 kg retail) and
  white maize grain (3.5 kg retail). Prices are in SSP.
