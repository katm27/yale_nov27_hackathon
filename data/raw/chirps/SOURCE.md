# CHIRPS v3.0 monthly rainfall

Per-month Africa GeoTIFFs (0.05°, mm/month), 2018-01..2025-12:

    https://data.chc.ucsb.edu/products/CHIRPS/v3.0/monthly/africa/tifs/chirps-v3.0.YYYY.MM.tif

The ~5 MB Africa files are cached in `_cache/` (not committed). `chirps_v3_ssd_2018_2025.nc` holds the crop to bbox W23.3 S3.3 E36.1 N12.4 for all months (re-run with `--no-download`).

Citation: Funk, C. et al. (2015) Sci. Data 2:150066; CHIRPS v3.0 (Climate Hazards Center, UCSB, 2025).
