# Soil — scripts/static_pipeline.py
ISRIC SoilGrids 2.0 (250 m, Homolosine) read directly from https://files.isric.org/soilgrids/latest/data/<prop>/<prop>_<depth>_mean.vrt
(clay, sand, soc, phh2o; depths 0-5, 5-15, 15-30 cm) and wrb/MostProbable.vrt, decimated x4 (~1 km). Nothing stored in repo.
Copernicus DEM GLO-90 from Planetary Computer (`cop-dem-glo-90`) for elevation.
