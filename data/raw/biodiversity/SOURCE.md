# Species richness — scripts/static_pipeline.py
BiodiversityMapping.org 10 km richness GeoTIFFs (Eckert IV), package 2019-03-14 (revised 2020-02):
https://biodiversitymapping.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/BiodiversityMapping_TIFFs_2019_03d14_revised.zip (14.6 MB, `bm_tiffs.zip`).
Based on IUCN range maps (amphibians Dec 2017, mammals Mar 2018) and BirdLife v7 (birds). Cite: Jenkins, Pimm & Joppa (2013) PNAS; biodiversitymapping.org.
`extracted/` is git-ignored (unzipped copy).
